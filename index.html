Who We Are

Anakainosis is the Greek word for renewal — Romans 12:2. We exist to call a generation back to the altar, to prayer that consecrates, and to a life wholly yielded to God.

Our Vision

To see men set on fire by God Himself, renewed in mind, and sent to the nations.

Our Mission

To raise a praying, consecrated people through teaching, prayer altars, and apostolic impartation.

Our Mandate

Prayer is not for vanity — it is for consecration. We ask for God, because God is the Treasure.

“Whatever things you ask when you pray, believe that you receive them, and you will have them.”

Mark 11:24 NKJV
